# Quantum Measurement and Relational Conservation in TCGS–SEQUENTION

**Henry Arellano-Peña**

Companion repository for *Quantum Measurement and Relational Conservation in TCGS–SEQUENTION: Invariants, Preparation, and Conditional Readout*.

## Scientific scope

The manuscript distinguishes preservation of a conserved charge-sector relation from invariance of a probability distribution after conditioning on a measurement outcome. Its operator results are independent of the TCGS–SEQUENTION interpretation. The framework interpretation is not presented as independent experimental confirmation or as a derivation of the quantum instrument from four ontological axioms.

## Distribution policy

The repository contains publication-facing documents, executable Python verification code, numerical results, individual reference information, and authorial scope clarifications. LaTeX source files, Overleaf archives, and internal compilations are excluded.
